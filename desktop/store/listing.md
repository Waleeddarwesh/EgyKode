## Description

*(Up to 10,000 characters)*

> **Learn Cloud & DevOps by building one real production platform.**
>
> Not a pile of disconnected tutorials. One system — provisioned with Terraform,
> configured with Ansible, running on Kubernetes, delivered by GitOps, observed
> end to end. You build it phase by phase.
>
> **What is inside**
>
> • 57 chapters, ordered from Linux and networking through to SRE
> • 114 hands-on labs — guided walkthroughs, self-directed challenges, and
>   incident labs where you get a symptom and no solution
> • Roadmaps that say which chapter comes next, and why
> • A capstone project you build and operate yourself
>
> **How it teaches**
>
> Every chapter opens by saying why you are reading it now and what the previous
> phase did not solve. Every technology chapter covers not just how to use it but
> how it fails, how to diagnose it, and what it costs. Where a lab helps, it is
> linked from the exact section that teaches the material.
>
> **On Windows**
>
> • Read offline — pages you open are kept, and you can save a phase before you
>   travel
> • Opens like an app, with the curriculum and labs a keystroke away
> • Same content as egykode.com, always — corrections reach you without an update
>
> **Free, and open source.** No account required to read. No advertising. No
> paid tier.
>
> Some features need a connection: running a lab in a browser terminal, anything
> that talks to a cloud provider, and community pages.

## URLs

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Website | `https://egykode.com` |
| Privacy policy | `https://egykode.com/privacy/` |
| Support contact | `https://github.com/Waleeddarwesh/EgyKode/issues` |

Partner Center requires a working privacy policy URL, and a submission with a
404 there is rejected — the most common reason a first submission fails. The
policy exists at `/privacy/` and is verified by `npm run desktop:check`.

**Support is the repository's issue tracker rather than a personal email**, on
purpose: a Store listing is public, and an address published there cannot be
withdrawn. The privacy policy already points to the same place. Change it to an
email in Partner Center if you would rather, knowing it becomes public.
